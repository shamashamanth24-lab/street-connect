Frequently asked questions

Is StreetConnect an official government portal?

No. It is a student demo project and is not affiliated with a municipal authority or government department.

Does registration here give me a vending certificate?

No. This form only creates a local demo profile. Contact your local authority for official registration.

Where can I check PM SVANidhi details?

Visit the official PM SVANidhi portal linked above for current information and eligibility.
